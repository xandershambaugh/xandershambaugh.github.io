Lamps

Designer / Postmodern Tectonics / 2024
Los Angeles, CA

Sculptural paper lamp — illuminated on bookshelf.

Overview

A series of handmade sculptural lamps exploring the intersection of paper craft, light, and architectural form. Each lamp is constructed from folded and shaped paper panels supported by minimal wire armatures, creating organic, luminous forms that cast warm diffused light. The process-driven approach draws from architectural modelmaking techniques applied at the scale of lighting design.
